Free Android Auto on BMW NBT Evo

Native Android Auto on the iDrive 6 screen of BMWs, MINIs and the Toyota GR Supra, wireless, with the car's controller and speakers. No activation to buy: it is free.

Where it comes from

The Android Auto app is NBT-AAD, by nanaisu and its contributors, packaged as an NBT Manager app (nf-android-auto). NBT-AAD's licence allows noncommercial use only: you never need to pay anyone for it.

What you get

  • Wireless: over the car's Wi-Fi Direct by default, or the CarPlay hotspot. Over USB as a beta option.
  • The car's controls: the iDrive controller and the buttons around it reach Android Auto.
  • The car's speakers (native audio), or Bluetooth audio, as you choose.
  • Stays out of the way: the reverse camera, park assist and screens the car opens come first; calls keep Android Auto on screen.
  • Settings from the phone: car make (BMW, MINI, Toyota), Wi-Fi network, audio, turn arrows in the cluster and head-up display, the phone's GPS, the favourites bar, changed in the NBT Manager app.
  • Updates from the phone: new NBT-AAD versions arrive in Get apps, checked by the car, and roll back by themselves if they do not start.

How to install it

  • Check your car: NBT Evo with iDrive 6.
  • Install NBT Manager once, with an ENET cable, and pair your phone.
  • In the phone app, open Get apps › Android Auto › Install, and confirm on iDrive.
  • Start it from App Manager, then connect your phone as the app's settings say.

Already running NBT-AAD installed by hand? Remove that install first: the app refuses to start while another copy of NBT-AAD runs.

Status

Beta. Report problems in the discussions with your model and the app's log, which the phone app reads from the car. It is a test build: use it only while parked.
