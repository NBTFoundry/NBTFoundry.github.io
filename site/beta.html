Google Play · closed testing

Test the phone app

NBT Manager for Android is in closed testing on Google Play. Anyone can join, with the Google account your phone uses for Play.

  1. Join the group

    Open the testers' group and press Join group. Play lets its members in.

    Open the group

  2. Become a tester

    Open the test's page and press Become a tester. If it says the app is unavailable, wait a few minutes after joining.

    Open the test's page

  3. Install

    Install NBT Manager from Google Play. Updates come from Play too.

    Get it on Google Play

After the install

  • New here? Follow the install guide to set up the car and pair the phone.
  • Had the app from a test kit or built from source? Uninstall it first, then pair your cars again: Play's copy is signed with another key.

If Play shows an error

  • "Check your internet connection", "Something went wrong" or the install fails: Play has not caught up with your tester status yet. Try again in a few hours: it clears by itself.
  • Still nothing? Play must use the account that joined the group: check the avatar at the top right of Play.
  • "Can't install": a copy from a test kit may remain in a work profile, Private space or Secure Folder. Uninstall it there too.

Good to know

  • Test builds can break. Report problems in the phone app's issues.
  • To leave, open the test's page again and press Leave the program.
  • Privacy: what the app keeps and sends.
