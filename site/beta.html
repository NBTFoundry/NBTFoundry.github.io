Test the phone app

NBT Manager for Android is in closed testing on Google Play. Anyone can join, with the Google account your phone uses for Play.

1. Join the group

  • Open groups.google.com/g/nbtfoundry-testers and press Join group.

2. Become a tester

  • Open play.google.com/apps/testing/org.nbtfoundry.manager and press Become a tester. If it says the app is unavailable, wait a few minutes after joining.

3. Install

  • Install NBT Manager from Google Play. Updates come from Play.
  • Already have the app from a test kit or built from source? Uninstall it first, then pair your cars again: Play's copy is signed with another key.
  • Then follow the install guide.

Good to know

  • Test builds can break. Report problems on github.com/NBTFoundry/mobile-app/issues.
  • To leave, open the link in step 2 and press Leave the program.
  • What the app keeps and sends: privacy.
