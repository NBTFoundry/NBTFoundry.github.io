Does my BMW have NBT Evo?

NBTFoundry's apps run on BMW's NBT Evo head unit with iDrive 6. The home screen tells you in a second.

The check

  • Supported: the home screen shows large tiles side by side (map, media, phone…). That is iDrive 6 on NBT Evo.
  • Not yet: a vertical list of entries with a preview beside it. That is the earlier NBT, CIC or an NBT Evo still on the older ID4 menu.
  • Not supported: the curved or widescreen Live Cockpit of 2019 and later (iDrive 7 and newer, MGU), and the smaller entry units without NBT Evo.

Diagnostic and coding tools show the head unit's software name: on NBT Evo it starts with NBTevo_, for example NBTevo_Y21432J.

Models

NBT Evo with iDrive 6 was fitted from about 2016 to 2019. Years vary by model and market, so the screen check above is the reliable one. NBT Evo is found in, among others:

  • BMW F series, facelift years: 1 Series (F20, F21), 2 Series (F22, F23), 3 Series (F30, F31, F34), 4 Series (F32, F33, F36), X5 and X6 (F15, F16).
  • BMW G series, early years: 5 Series (G30, G31), 7 Series (G11, G12), X3 (G01).
  • MINI with navigation (F54 to F60).
  • Toyota GR Supra (A90, J29), which uses the same head unit with Toyota's menus. We have not run it on a Supra ourselves yet: Supra owners' reports are very welcome.
  • Retrofits: an NBT Evo fitted later to an older BMW works the same once it runs iDrive 6.

What else you need

  • An Android phone (no iPhone app).
  • An ENET cable and a computer for the first install: the steps.

Not sure?

Ask in the discussions with your model, year and a photo of the home screen. Reports of cars that work, or do not, help everyone.
