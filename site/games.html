Guide

Doom and retro games on your iDrive screen

While you wait in the car park: classic games on the NBT Evo screen, through the car's speakers, with the iDrive controller or a USB gamepad.

The games

  • Doom (PrBoom+): the 1993 shooter, with its shareware episode included.
  • Super Nintendo (Snes9x).
  • PlayStation (PCSX ReARMed).
  • Mega Drive, Mega-CD, 32X and Master System (PicoDrive).
  • Arcade (FinalBurn Neo), from zipped romsets.

No game, ROM or BIOS is included apart from Doom's shareware episode: use only games you have the right to use.

Controls

  • The iDrive controller: turn, push, tilt, and the buttons around it. Enough for menus and many games.
  • A USB gamepad in the car's USB socket: common pads work, with buttons laid out by position, so A is where you expect it on each pad.
  • Each app's pause menu has saves, states and its settings.

Your games

  • Send them from the phone app, or import them from a USB stick in the car.
  • Saves stay on the car, per app.

Get started

  • Check your car: NBT Evo with iDrive 6.
  • Install NBT Manager once, then pick the games in the phone app's Apps tab.

Only while parked. Show your setup in the discussions.
