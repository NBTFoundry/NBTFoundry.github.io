Get started

One cable install, then everything from the phone

Read the warning first: changing a head unit's software is at your own risk.

  • An ENET cableIt joins the car's diagnostic socket, under the dashboard, to a computer's network port. About 15 to 25 € online.
  • A computer, onceWindows, macOS or Linux, for the first install only.
  • An Android phoneFor apps, updates and settings, over the car's own Wi-Fi.

  1. Install NBT Manager

    Once per car. Plug in the cable, open the desktop installer, press Connect, then Install. It asks before it changes anything on the car.

  2. Pair your phone

    Open the NBT Manager app, scan the code on the car's screen and compare six digits.

    The app is coming to Google Play. Today it is built from source; announcements will say when it is out.

  3. Install apps

    Open Get apps, pick one, install. The car checks it and asks you to confirm on iDrive. A bad update rolls back by itself.

FAQ

Questions before you start

What does it cost?

Nothing. NBT Manager and every app are free and open source, with no account, no ads and no tracking. You only buy the cable.

Which cars does it work on?

Cars with BMW's NBT Evo head unit and iDrive 6, whose home screen shows large tiles side by side: many BMW F and G series from about 2016 to 2019, MINI with navigation and the Toyota GR Supra. Models and details.

Does it change iDrive?

It adds one entry, App Manager, to the iDrive menu. Your apps start from there; it replaces nothing BMW put on the screen.

Can I undo it?

Yes. Each app is removed from the phone, and the desktop installer removes NBT Manager itself.

Can it damage my car?

It can. Changing a head unit's software can brick it, and recovery may need professional service at your expense. App updates roll back by themselves, but nothing here is risk free: read the warning.

What about my warranty?

Modifying the head unit may affect manufacturer support or warranty coverage, depending on where you live. If that matters to you, do not install it.

Can I use the apps while driving?

No. Use them only while parked.

Are games included?

Only Doom's shareware episode. For the emulators you bring games you have the right to use, from the phone or a USB stick.

Does it work with an iPhone?

No. The phone app is for Android only.

Is this from BMW?

No. NBTFoundry is an independent open-source project, not affiliated with or endorsed by BMW.

Where do I get help?

In the discussions: questions, ideas and photos of your car are welcome.

Developers

Apps in minutes

Open the template, start your coding agent and tell it what you want on the screen. It already knows the SDK, the iDrive controller, gamepads and the car's speakers: the skills that teach it come with the SDK. The compiler is a free download for Linux, macOS and Windows. Your app runs on your desktop first, and the same code builds for the car.

Have an old game or emulator you love? Point the agent at its source code. Doom and four emulators reached the car this way.
