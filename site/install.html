Install apps on a BMW NBT Evo, step by step

One cable install with a computer, then apps, updates and settings from your Android phone. No command line, no SSH client, no files to copy by hand.

What you need

  • A car with NBT Evo and iDrive 6: the home screen shows large tiles side by side. Check your car. The Toyota GR Supra (A90) has the same head unit, and nobody has run this on one yet: Supra owners, tell us how it goes.
  • An ENET cable (OBD to Ethernet, about 15 to 25 €), or a Wi-Fi ENET adapter to do it from the phone.
  • A computer, once: Windows, macOS or Linux, with an internet connection.
  • An Android phone with the NBT Manager app.
  • A battery charger on the car, recommended: the ignition stays on for a while.

1. Connect

  • Park, engine off, ignition on. Wait for iDrive to finish starting.
  • Plug the ENET cable into the OBD socket under the dashboard and into the computer.
  • Download the desktop installer and press Connect. It finds the head unit on the cable by itself.

2. Enable SSH, if asked

Most head units do not accept SSH out of the box. When the connection is refused, the installer offers to enable it: it finds the car's gateway and relays an activation service's diagnostic commands to it. This needs the computer's internet connection and your agreement; then it connects again. SSH is needed only for this first install: afterwards the phone does everything.

3. Install NBT Manager

  • Press Install. The installer checks the signed release, shows what it will change and asks first.
  • When it asks Restart the head unit?, accept: iDrive restarts, like after switching the car off and on.
  • App Manager now appears in the iDrive menu, next to the other apps.

4. Pair your phone

  • In iDrive, open App Manager, then Settings › Pair a phone. The car shows a Wi-Fi code.
  • In the NBT Manager phone app, press Pair a car, scan the code and compare the six digits on both screens.

The phone app is coming to Google Play; today it is built from source.

5. Install apps

  • Open Get apps on the phone, pick an app, press Install.
  • The car checks the package and asks you to confirm on iDrive. Nothing installs without that.
  • Start it from App Manager. Use apps only while parked.

Later: updates and removal

  • Updates of apps and of NBT Manager itself come from the phone, without the cable. A version that does not start and stay healthy is replaced by the previous one.
  • Logs and crash reports are read from the phone, to share when something goes wrong.
  • Removal: each app from the phone; NBT Manager itself with the desktop installer.

Something went wrong?

Ask in the discussions with your model, year and what the installer said. Before you start, read the warning: changing a head unit's software is at your own risk.
