Privacy

NBT Manager for Android (org.nbtfoundry.manager). Updated November 23, 2026.

Nothing collected

The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. NBTFoundry receives no data from it.

Kept on your phone

  • Your paired cars: their names and addresses, and the phone's signing key for each, which stays in the Android Keystore and never leaves the phone.
  • Your library of application packages, your language and beta preferences.
  • Logs and crash reports you read from a car. They leave the phone only when you share them.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. The app is excluded from Android backups.

Connections

  • Your car, over its own Wi-Fi, when you pair, read, install or change something.
  • GitHub, when you open Get apps or download from it: the catalog from nbtfoundry.github.io and packages from github.com. GitHub sees your IP address, as for any web request (GitHub's privacy statement).

The app makes no other connection.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan the Wi-Fi code on the car's screen. No picture is stored or sent.
  • Wi-Fi and nearby devices: to join the car's Wi-Fi. On Android 12 and earlier, Android requires the location permission for this; the app never reads your location.
